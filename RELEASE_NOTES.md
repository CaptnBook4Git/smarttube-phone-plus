SmartTube+: a YouTube client for Android phones and tablets, built on SmartTube.

| APK | For |
|---|---|
| SmartTube-Phone-arm64.apk (or StreamTube-arm64.apk) | Almost every phone (64-bit ARM) |
| SmartTube-Phone-arm32.apk (or StreamTube-arm32.apk) | Older or budget phones with a 32-bit processor |
| SmartTube-Phone-universal.apk (or StreamTube-universal.apk) | Any device, also x86 emulators and Chromebooks |

What's new
- Video Chapters in Comments: Filter comments by chapter with a new chapter dropdown above the comments list
- Live Chapter Tracking: Choose "Live: Current chapter" to automatically update the comments view as the video plays from one chapter to the next
- Timestamp-based Filtering: Chapter view shows only comments containing timestamps within that chapter's interval, hiding comments without timestamps
- Chapter Selection: Browse comments of any specific chapter without changing or seeking the current video playback
- Video Chapters on Player: Live chapter title indicator next to the playback time, interactive chapter picker dialog, and seekbar segment markers
